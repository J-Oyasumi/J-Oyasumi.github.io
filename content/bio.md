I am Han Jiang (蒋涵). I am currently a third-year undergraduate student at [Nanjing University](https://www.nju.edu.cn/en/), majoring in Intelligence Science and Technology.

My research interests lie in robot learning for manipulation. More specifically, two questions motivate me: (1) how can we effectively leverage heterogeneous data, such as human video, for scalable robot learning? and (2) how can we enable robots to perform dexterous, long-horizon tasks beyond pick-and-place?

My long-term goal is to bring robots out of the laboratory and into everyday human life, where they can support people in their daily routines and gradually grow into trustworthy companions that meaningfully improve how we live and work.
